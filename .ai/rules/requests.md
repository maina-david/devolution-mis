# Requests

## Preserve governed community moderation evidence
Discussion subscriptions must remain county/portfolio scoped and notify only active subscribers other than the contributor. Moderators cannot decide their own posts; hide/restore decisions require an attributed rationale and must preserve original content for audit. Ordinary participants must never receive hidden post content.

## Use the shared workflow for community abuse reports
Community reports require county/portfolio scope, active-duplicate prevention, and the published moderation workflow with reporter/triager/decision-maker separation. Final decisions must record a resolution and explicit hide or keep-visible action while preserving the original post and linking its moderation evidence to the report. Never allow the post author to adjudicate the report.
